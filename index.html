03 // Friction Engineering

Friction Catcher: Detecting the Break ↗

Most feedback tools interrupt users indiscriminately. This one doesn't. I designed and shipped a behaviour-detecting micro-widget that applies signal confidence theory — firing only when there is objective evidence of frustration. Rage clicking qualifies. Reading slowly does not. The ethical line is the product decision.

About

// Who is Rithwick?

// SCENE 01 — WHO

Product Designer. CS undergrad. at IIITDM Kancheepuram. I work on systems, interfaces and AI-assisted workflows that make everyday tasks simpler and easier to use. Based in Chennai.

I usually start by finding friction in a product, breaking down what feels inefficient or unclear, redesigning the flow, and shipping a working proof. That's the loop.

Outside product work, I spend time experimenting with visual grammar, filmmaking, editing, and design systems - mostly driven by curiosity and the love of making things. That side lives on the Creativity page.
