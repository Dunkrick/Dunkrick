About

// Who is Rithwick?

(the person behind the work)

CS undergrad at IIITDM Kancheepuram, based in Chennai. I spend most of my time trying to understand how systems work - products, data pipelines, user flows, visual narratives - and then building things that make those systems clearer, simpler, or more honest.

My loop: find friction → map the system → redesign the flow → ship working proof. I've done this with e-commerce platforms, growth analytics dashboards, and interfaces that needed fewer decisions, not more features.

I also make films. Not because it's related to product work - but because editing a film and editing an interface require the same instinct: knowing what to cut.
