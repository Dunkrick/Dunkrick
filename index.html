About

// Who is Rithwick?

(the person behind the work)

Product. CS undergrad. at IIITDM Kancheepuram. I work on systems, interfaces and AI-assisted workflows that make everyday tasks simpler and easier to use. Based in Chennai.

I usually start by finding friction in a product, breaking down what feels inefficient or unclear, redesigning the flow, and shipping a working proof. That's the loop.

Outside product work, I spend time experimenting with visual grammar, filmmaking, editing, and design systems - mostly driven by curiosity and the love of making things. That side lives on the Creativity page.
