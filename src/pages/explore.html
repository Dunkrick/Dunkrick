About Studio

Philosophy

Create like a child

Follow curiosity without permission. Make things that don't need to justify themselves. The best creative work starts with "what if?" not "what for?"

Discipline

Craft like a scientist

But ship it. Deadlines force decisions. Constraints create clarity. The craft is in the edit — knowing what to cut, what to keep, what to refine.

Practice

Public learning

Studio is where I think in public. The in-progress, the failed, the "maybe someday." If it's here, it's real — even if it's not done.
