Every complex system starts with a single decision. For Dream Wall, the first architectural decision (ED-001) was choosing the foundation.

I standardized on Node.js. Why? It's simple, it's everywhere, and it allows me to keep the mental model consistent between the frontend and backend. 

The goal here isn't to over-engineer on day one. It's about getting the environment ready for deployment. The initial setup is just a skeleton, but it's *my* skeleton.
